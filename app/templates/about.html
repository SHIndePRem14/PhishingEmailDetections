About PhishGuard

AI-powered phishing email detection system
About the Project

PhishGuard is an AI-powered phishing email detection system designed to identify potentially fraudulent, malicious, or suspicious emails.

The system combines Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), keyword analysis, and URL-based security heuristics to analyze the content of an email and determine whether it is Phishing or Legitimate.

It is designed as a cybersecurity project to provide users with a simple and effective way to analyze suspicious emails without opening potentially dangerous links or attachments.

The main machine learning component of PhishGuard uses TF-IDF and Logistic Regression.

TF-IDF converts the subject and body of an email into numerical features based on the importance of words and terms. These features are then provided to the Logistic Regression classifier, which predicts whether the email is phishing or legitimate.

Along with the ML prediction, the system can identify suspicious keywords, URLs, urgency-related language, account-verification requests, password-related requests, and other common phishing indicators.

PhishGuard never opens, executes, or downloads links/attachments from analyzed emails.
